Key Methodologies

Three core frameworks that guide professional advisory.

Consulting is not about listing software features. It is a systematic mapping of business problems to structured digital workflows.

1. The SPICED Discovery Model Using HSL tailored models (Situation, Problem, Impact, Critical Event, Decision) to extract not just what the lead wants, but the financial and organizational impact of failing to change.
2. B2B Customer Journey Map (CJM) Managing B2B accounts through a rigorous 6-stage lifecycle: Nhận diện (Awareness), Khảo sát (Discovery), Demo (System Mapping), Dùng thử (Sandbox Proof), Đàm phán & Ký (Closing), and After Sales (Onboarding & Adoption).
3. Transition-Lane Qualification Enforcing strict rules of engagement to protect consulting margins: identifying when standard B2B Mass SaaS leads cross lanes into complex Enterprise projects requiring deep custom integrations or bespoke configurations.
Applied Cases & Tooling

Practical value-mapping verticals and advanced workflows.

Below are three mock customer verticals showing how granular pain points are mapped to platform modules, alongside our AI-augmented consulting methods.

Value-Mapping Case Verticals

Sovi (Manufacturing Segment) Pain Point: High shift-scheduling errors and labor-intensive timesheet calculations for multi-floor manufacturing plants.
Value Map: Mapped scheduling rules to 1HRM Shift Management and automated payroll calculations, while using 1WORK Document Management to store safety certifications, reducing weekly payroll prep work.
GHN (Logistics & Delivery Segment) Pain Point: Delays in fleet communication and manual handoffs in delivery fulfillment workflows violating SLAs.
Value Map: Standardized operational flows via 1WORK Business Process Automation (BPA) and synced client interaction histories inside 1CRM, improving fleet response latency and delivery tracking visibility.
Pizza 4P's (F&B / Retail Segment) Pain Point: Floor staffing shortages caused by rapid, unmonitored shift-swapping and seasonal payroll volatility.
Value Map: Leveraged 1HRM Digital Timesheets for instant supervisor sign-offs, connected to 1WORK's internal chat to keep managers updated, securing shift compliance and operational stability.

Advisory Reflections & Tools

The Onboarding Learning Curve A crucial milestone in the consultant onboarding was bridging the Week 1 Discovery Gap (understanding customer pain points) with the Week 2 Platform Architecture (mastering system configuration, database structures, and module dependencies). True advisory connects these two halves.
AI-Augmented Consulting Workflows Designed structured prompt systems to optimize diagnostic preparation. By feeding client industry sectors, size, and suspected friction points into AI tools, we generated specialized discovery questionnaires, simulated difficult client objections, and drafted personalized value-mapping proposals.
Standard vs. Transition Rules Standard B2B Mass SaaS focuses on quick setup, high-frequency repeated tasks, and minimal customization. When clients request deep custom integrations or legacy database migrations, they are qualified out of Mass and routed to Enterprise lanes, preserving consulting efficiency.
