VIEN

Arts workshop participation

A survey at Ladder Art Space examined how learning, social and enjoyment values related to attitudes toward a workshop and intentions to participate.

Explore the findings ↓Methods & sources ↗
Sample
216 valid responses
Context
Ladder Art Space, Melbourne
Analysis
Learning, attitude + intention

Learning value led

Strongest reported link to positive workshop attitudes.

Examined how learning, social experience and enjoyment at Ladder Art Space related to attitudes and participation intention.

What a workshop team could test

Test clearer learning goals, beginner guidance and feedback. The survey has not established an increase in bookings.

What the study does not establish

The survey measured intention at one point in time. It did not establish an increase in bookings or repeat attendance.

How the study was conducted

Survey analysis · Scale validation · Mediation analysis · PROCESS Model 4

  1. 1

    Check the questions

    Check that the questions measure the intended workshop experiences and attitudes.

  2. 2

    Examine the link

    PROCESS Model 4 examines whether attitude helps connect experience value with intention to participate. This is called mediation.

  3. 3

    Plan a real-world test

    Beginner guidance is a proposed service test. Measuring actual bookings would require new data.
