Service experience · Melbourne

Arts workshop participation

A survey at Ladder Art Space examined how learning, social and enjoyment values related to attitudes toward a workshop and intentions to participate.

Survey research project

Explore findings ↓
Sample
216 valid responses
Context
Ladder Art Space, Melbourne
Analysis
Mediation model

What makes a workshop worth joining?

The analysis examined experience values, workshop attitude and participation intention. Measurement checks and PROCESS Model 4 were used to examine the mediation pathway.

Learning value connects with attitude

The project materials report learning value as the strongest signal for attitude. The reported paths are 0.35 from learning value to attitude and 0.59 from attitude to intention.

Workshop service design

The findings suggest testing clear learning goals, beginner guidance and feedback during the workshop. These are service-design proposals rather than measured attendance gains.

The cross-sectional data measures intention. It does not prove that these changes cause bookings or repeat attendance.
