Consumer research · Vietnam

EV choice experiment

A choice experiment with young consumers in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City examined how range, charging, network size and body design affect EV preferences.

CIEMB 2025 manuscript

Explore findings ↓
Sample
212 consumers
Design
Stated-choice experiment
Analysis
Two-class latent model

Which trade-offs shape an EV choice?

Participants compared vehicle profiles with different features. Multinomial and mixed logit models were compared with a latent class model to examine differences between buyer groups.

Two ways to value an EV

The reported model separates a utility-focused group (65%) from a design-sensitive group (35%). Range has the highest marginal willingness-to-pay estimate in both groups. Design estimates are less certain.

Product positioning

The results can inform product positioning and the features emphasized for each group. They do not establish demand at a particular retail price.

These are stated preferences and model estimates. Several attribute confidence intervals include zero; the estimated willingness to pay is not a sales result.
