Hotel service priorities

An analysis of hotel reviews examined whether poor service and good service had equal effects on review scores.

Explore the findings ↓Methods & sources ↗
Data
97,014 reviews
Period
Jan 2023–Jan 2025
Analysis
Comparing praise and complaints

Cleanliness and staff praise

Linked to the largest score decreases and increases.

Compared how praise and complaints about eight hotel-service topics related to review scores.

What a hotel team could learn

Distinguish service failures to prevent from improvements that may help a hotel stand out.

What the study does not establish

The results link review language with review scores. They do not show how much revenue a service investment would generate.

How the study was conducted

Text analysis · Sentence-BERT · OLS regression · PRCA · Kano model

  1. 1

    Sort the comments

    Sentence-BERT, a text-analysis model, matches sentences to eight hotel service attributes.

  2. 2

    Compare praise and complaints

    Examine positive and negative comments separately to see how each relates to review scores.

  3. 3

    Interpret the pattern

    The reported categories distinguish expected basics, services where better performance helps, and extras that can delight guests.
