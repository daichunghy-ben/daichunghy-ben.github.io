Which service failures cost the most?

Review text was mapped to eight attributes with Sentence-BERT. Positive and negative sentiment indicators entered a regression to compare reward and penalty coefficients.

Cleanliness and staff service have different roles

Cleanliness has the largest reported penalty when it fails. Staff service has the largest positive reward coefficient. The model groups attributes into basic, performance and excitement categories.

Study plan +
Question
Do service priorities survive annotation error, property dependence and a later review period?
Design & unit
Review-level reanalysis with property identifiers and a reserved temporal sample.
Measures & analysis
Report mapping and sentiment errors, coefficient intervals, threshold sensitivity and ranking stability. Keep mapping quality separate from regression fit.
Data & sample
Requires sentence-level labels, rating data, property IDs and collection dates. Size the annotation set for rare attributes as well as frequent ones.
Decision gate
Recommend a priority only when the direction and practical size remain stable. Investment returns require a separate service evaluation.

Service investment priorities

Hotel teams can use the comparison to distinguish failures that need attention from improvements that may support differentiation.

Regression coefficients describe associations with review scores. They do not establish the revenue return from investing in an attribute.

Methods & sources +
Dataset
The project materials report 97,014 Booking.com reviews from approximately 1,000 Vietnamese properties, collected for Jan 2023–Jan 2025.
Model
OLS with HC1 robust standard errors; R² = 0.220. Mean review score: 8.83/10. Eight service attributes were classified.
Classification
Basic: cleanliness and WiFi. Performance: staff, room quality, value and location. Excitement: facilities and food.
