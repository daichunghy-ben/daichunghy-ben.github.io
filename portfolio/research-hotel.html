Hospitality research

Hotel customer segmentation

Guest reviews reveal different priorities within the same hotel market. This study grouped Da Nang guests by the service attributes they discussed and how they rated them.

Analysis project

Data

Coverage
458 Da Nang hotels
Method
Sentiment analysis + clustering

What do different guests value?

Overall ratings can hide differences between guests. Review text was mapped to hotel attributes, scored for sentiment and grouped with K-means. A Gaussian mixture model provided a robustness check.

Ten groups within the review data

The largest reported group, Minimalists, accounts for 43.7% of the sample. Smaller groups focus on dining, exploration, service and other parts of the stay.

Study plan +
Question
Do the same service priorities remain visible in unseen hotels and later reviews?
Design & unit
Model-development study using reviews nested within hotels; retain hotel and date identifiers for validation.
Measures & analysis
Audit aspect and sentiment labels, benchmark feature representations and clustering, then report stability and uncertain assignments.
Data & sample
Requires the review matrix, collection dates, feature codebook and reuse permission. Set annotation size from class coverage and target precision; do not treat 10 clusters as a fixed requirement.
Decision gate
Extend only if the patterns are stable and interpretable in the reserved data. A service pilot needs its own outcome, comparison and sample rationale.

Service priorities

The segmentation gives hotel teams a way to compare guest priorities before choosing service improvements or messages for different audiences.

Segments describe the review sample. Their shares should not be treated as hotel occupancy, market share or measured revenue effects.

Methods & sources +
Dataset
Project materials report 74,896 Booking.com reviews across 458 Da Nang hotels. This is the dataset for this segmentation study.
Analysis
Attribute-level sentiment analysis (ABSA), K-means clustering and a Gaussian mixture robustness check. The materials report 108,906 sentences and 148,846 extracted aspect–sentence pairs.
Evidence
Dataset counts and segment shares are reported in the project materials; they have not been independently verified here.
