Hospitality research

Hotel customer segmentation

Grouped hotel-review patterns into guest profiles and compared the service attributes each profile valued.

Review analysis · Aspect-level sentiment · K-means & GMM

Output

Review features, ten reported profiles and recommendations for hotel service planning.

Analysis project

Data
74,896 reviews
Coverage
458 Da Nang hotels
Method
Sentiment analysis + clustering

What do different guests value?

Overall ratings can hide differences between guests. Review text was mapped to hotel attributes, scored for sentiment and grouped with K-means. A Gaussian mixture model provided a robustness check.

Ten clusters. Eight attributes.

Compare the service attributes.

Read down a column to compare sentiment across clusters. Read across a row to compare aspects of the stay.

Sentiment: −1 negative · 0 neutral · +1 positive

Sentiment is one part of a service decision.

Combine the tone of reviews with how often an attribute is mentioned and what a hotel can change. A positive color alone does not establish a service priority.

Ten groups within the review data

The largest reported group, Minimalists, accounts for 43.7% of the sample. Smaller groups focus on dining, exploration, service and other parts of the stay.

How the analysis works

Language layer

108,906 sentences yielded 148,846 reported aspect–sentence pairs.

Feature layer

Attribute sentiment represents what was discussed and how it was evaluated.

Clustering

K-means forms the reported groups; GMM is the reported robustness check.

Model specification

Review text → attribute features → clustering → profile interpretation

Sentiment and salience answer different questions: how an attribute is evaluated and how much it is mentioned.

Research methods

Language analysis builds the features; clustering tests how reviews group together.

Data

Review text

74,896 reviews across 458 Da Nang hotels. The unit represented in the segmentation is a review, not a verified unique guest.

Language analysis

Aspect sentiment

Map sentences to hotel attributes and score sentiment at that level. Project materials report 148,846 aspect–sentence pairs; they do not identify a specific ABSA checkpoint.

Primary model

K-means

Group reviews by similarity in attribute-level features. Ten reported groups form the displayed segmentation; the portfolio does not contain the full selection diagnostics.

Robustness model

Gaussian mixture

Check the segmentation with a probabilistic alternative. Agreement scores and covariance settings are not supplied, so this is a reported robustness step rather than a reproduced test.

Interpretation

Guest profiles

Compare what each group emphasizes, then name the profiles. The labels summarize priorities visible in the review features.

Proposed next study

Test whether the segments travel

Develop a reproducible NLP and clustering benchmark before applying profiles to new hotels.

Question
Do the same service priorities remain visible in unseen hotels and later reviews?
Design & unit
Model-development study using reviews nested within hotels; retain hotel and date identifiers for validation.
Measures & analysis
Audit aspect and sentiment labels, benchmark feature representations and clustering, then report stability and uncertain assignments.
Data & sample
Requires the review matrix, collection dates, feature codebook and reuse permission. Set annotation size from class coverage and target precision; do not treat 10 clusters as a fixed requirement.
Decision gate
Extend only if the patterns are stable and interpretable in the reserved data. A service pilot needs its own outcome, comparison and sample rationale.

Service priorities

The segmentation gives hotel teams a way to compare guest priorities before choosing service improvements or messages for different audiences.

Segments describe the review sample. Their shares should not be treated as hotel occupancy, market share or measured revenue effects.

Study notes & sources

Dataset
Project materials report 74,896 Booking.com reviews across 458 Da Nang hotels. This is the dataset for this segmentation study.
Analysis
Attribute-level sentiment analysis (ABSA), K-means clustering and a Gaussian mixture robustness check. The materials report 108,906 sentences and 148,846 extracted aspect–sentence pairs.
Evidence
Dataset counts and segment shares are reported in the project materials; they have not been independently verified here.
