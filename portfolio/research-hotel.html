Hospitality research

Hotel customer segmentation

Grouped hotel reviews by what guests discussed and whether their comments were positive or negative.

Customer review analysisText analysisCustomer grouping

Output

Ten review-based groups to help hotel teams compare guest feedback.

Analysis project

Data
74,896 reviews
Coverage
458 Da Nang hotels
Method
Review analysis + grouping

Research question

What do guests say about their stay?

An overall rating can hide different priorities. Review text shows which parts of a stay guests praise or criticize.

Ten review groups. Eight parts of a stay.

What did guests praise or criticize?

Each numbered row is a group of similar review patterns. Each column is one part of the stay, such as the room or staff service.

Review tone: −1 negative · 0 neutral · +1 positive

What should a hotel improve first?

Check what guests say, how often they mention it and what the hotel can change. Positive feedback alone does not tell a team where to invest first.

Key finding

Guests described ten different patterns

Ten review-based groups were reported. The largest, Minimalists, covered 43.7% of reviews. These are patterns in reviews, not confirmed types of people.

Practical use

How a hotel could use this

Compare comments and attribute results to decide which parts of the guest experience need closer attention.

Read with careThe shares describe this review sample. They do not measure hotel occupancy, market share or revenue.
