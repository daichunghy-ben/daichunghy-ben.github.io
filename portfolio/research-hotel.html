Hotel customer segmentation

Guest reviews reveal different priorities within the same hotel market. This study grouped Da Nang guests by the service attributes they discussed and how they rated them.

Method
Review analysis + grouping

How a hotel could use this

Compare comments and attribute results to decide which parts of the guest experience need closer attention.

What the study does not establish

The shares describe this review sample. They do not measure hotel occupancy, market share or revenue.

How the study was conducted

Text analysis · Aspect-based sentiment · K-means · Gaussian mixture · Customer segmentation

  1. 1

    Read the comments

    The materials report 108,906 sentences and 148,846 pairs linking a hotel attribute with a sentence.

  2. 2

    Describe the feedback

    Record which part of a stay is discussed and whether the comment is positive or negative.

  3. 3

    Group similar patterns

    K-means groups similar review patterns. A Gaussian mixture model (GMM) provides another way to check the grouping.
