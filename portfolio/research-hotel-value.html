Proposed research framework

This map is an interpretation framework linked to the segmentation study.

Source patterns

Review themes

Start with recurring themes from the Da Nang review analysis. This framework does not add an independent dataset.

Value dimensions

Comfort + care

Connect room comfort and service care to possible guest priorities. These dimensions provide an interpretation vocabulary, not estimated importance weights.

Value dimensions

Local experience

Connect food, location and ambience with what guests seek during a stay. Avoid assigning the same priority to every guest group.

Value dimension

Overall value

Relate price and the overall stay to value judgments. A theme alone does not identify willingness to pay.

Application

Service hypotheses

Translate themes into service or messaging options to test. A plausible guest story remains a hypothesis until measured.

Proposed next study

Turn the value framework into measurable constructs

Use qualitative work to define the dimensions before estimating a structural model.

Question
Are the proposed value dimensions distinct, understandable and comparable across guest groups?
Design & unit
Sequential qualitative instrument development and an independent guest survey.
Measures & analysis
Document definitions and item origins, evaluate measurement structure, then test a bounded association with a prespecified stay outcome.
Data & sample
Requires interviews, pilot feedback and new survey data. Plan qualitative coverage and quantitative precision separately; no new sample is claimed.
Decision gate
Use a structural model only after the measurement is defensible. Numerical weights and loyalty effects remain unestimated.
