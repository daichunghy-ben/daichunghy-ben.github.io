Framework to test

Three service concepts

Comfort · local experience · reliability.

Connects review themes with service ideas to discuss and test. It does not identify new customer groups.

How a hotel could use the examples

Discuss which guest needs a service addresses, then check the assumptions through interviews or a new study.

What the study does not establish

These profiles and service ideas are conceptual. Measured group sizes belong to the separate customer-segmentation study.

Research design

Research synthesis · Service design · Concept development

    Check with guests
