How can review themes guide a service decision?

Review themes are mapped to value dimensions such as comfort, service care and local experience. Guest stories then connect those themes with possible service and messaging choices.

Three ways to discuss guest value

Comfort seekers, local experience hunters and reliability-first travelers represent different service priorities. These are communication profiles for the framework, not additional measured segments.

Service design discussions

A hotel team can select a profile, review its priorities and use it as a starting point for a service-design discussion.

The profiles and actions are conceptual. Dataset counts and measured segment shares belong to the linked segmentation study.
