Virtual influencers and purchase intention

A survey experiment tested whether the message or the virtual creator's appeal affected purchase intention among Vietnamese Generation Z consumers.

Does the message or the creator persuade?

Participants saw one of four simulated Instagram posts. The experiment varied argument quality and peripheral appeal, then examined purchase intention and the moderating role of speciesism.

Creator appeal mattered, with a limit

Peripheral cues could affect purchase intention independently. Participants with stronger preferences for human endorsers reported lower purchase intention regardless of the virtual creator's attractiveness.

Study plan +
Question
Does clearer virtual-creator disclosure change behavior, and does the effect vary with human preference?
Design & unit
Preregistered factorial experiment with participants randomly assigned to matched posts.
Measures & analysis
Primary randomized contrast on behavior; secondary intention, trust and recognition. Specify interactions and multiplicity before data collection.
Data & sample
Requires new stimuli and participants. Use a smallest meaningful main effect and interaction to plan sample size; pilot the measures first.
Decision gate
Report the randomized contrast separately from measured mechanisms. Use real purchase claims only if transactions are observed.

Choosing a virtual creator

The result supports examining audience acceptance alongside creator design. Attractive visuals alone may not persuade audiences who strongly prefer human endorsers.

The experiment measured purchase intention after simulated content exposure. It did not measure purchases or campaign conversion.

Methods & sources +
Framework
Elaboration likelihood model: argument quality represents the central route; social attractiveness, physical attractiveness and attitude similarity represent peripheral cues.
Speciesism
The study uses speciesism to examine stronger preferences for human over non-human endorsers. It is a moderator, not an additional experimental condition.
