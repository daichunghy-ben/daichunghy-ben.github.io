VIEN

Influencer engagement and retention

A survey of active UGC-platform users examined how content experience, expectation alignment and brand equity relate to engagement and retention.

Explore the findings ↓Methods & sources ↗
Sample
565 users
Survey checks
200 users + 365 users
Method
Testing linked survey responses

Engagement linked the model

55% engagement · 70% retention variation explained.

Checked survey measures, then examined links between content experience, expectations, brand perceptions, engagement and retention.

What a campaign team could learn

Check whether content meets expectations, fits the brand and gives people a reason to interact.

What the study does not establish

A one-time survey cannot prove that a campaign change makes people return. The available materials do not include all model coefficients.

How the study was conducted

Survey analysis · EFA · CFA · SEM

  1. 1

    Explore the questions

    Exploratory factor analysis (EFA) uses 200 responses to examine which survey questions belong together.

  2. 2

    Check the measures

    Confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) uses 365 responses to check that measurement structure.

  3. 3

    Test the relationships

    Structural equation modelling (SEM) uses the same 365 responses to examine how the measured ideas relate to one another.
