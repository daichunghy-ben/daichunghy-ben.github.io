Influencer engagement and retention

A survey of active UGC-platform users examined how content experience, expectation alignment and brand equity relate to engagement and retention.

What sits between exposure and returning?

The sample was split between exploratory factor analysis and confirmatory measurement and structural analysis. The model tested engagement as the pathway linking the antecedent constructs with retention.

Engagement connects the model

The reported model explains 55% of engagement variance and 70% of retention variance. These are explanatory statistics, not increases in engagement or customer retention.

Study plan +
Question
Does earlier measured engagement predict later observed return behavior?
Design & unit
Consent-based longitudinal panel with participant-linked return events.
Measures & analysis
Evaluate measurement comparability, temporal structural relationships and future return prediction as distinct outputs.
Data & sample
Requires scale items, linked events, consent and a follow-up plan. Sample rationale must account for events, attrition and the proposed longitudinal model.
Decision gate
Do not call the mechanism causal without additional identification. Logged behavior and the original retention construct must be reported separately.
Grouped validation ↗Cross-validation guide ↗

Campaign evaluation

The model suggests examining content quality, expectation matching and creator–brand fit alongside the depth of audience interaction.

The cross-sectional survey does not prove that changing a campaign causes customers to return. Available materials do not include a complete public coefficient table.
