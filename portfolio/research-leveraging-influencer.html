Influencer engagement and retention

Examined a survey model of what encourages people to engage with content and keep using a platform.

Method

Research question

What might make an audience come back?

Checked survey measures, then examined links between content experience, expectations, brand perceptions, engagement and retention.

Key finding

Engagement connected the parts of the model

The model explained 55% of variation in engagement scores and 70% in retention scores. These are survey patterns, not campaign improvements.

Practical use

What a campaign team could learn

Check whether content meets expectations, fits the brand and gives people a reason to interact.

Read with careA one-time survey cannot prove that a campaign change makes people return. The available materials do not include all model coefficients.
