Psychological ownership at university

Separated individual and collective ownership and mapped their proposed links with commitment and cooperation.

Conceptual interpretation

How does ownership relate to commitment?

The framework connects control over work, identification with it and shared responsibility with organizational commitment. It outlines relationships to examine rather than reporting a new university dataset.

A pathway to investigate

Individual ownership concerns a person's connection with the work. Collective ownership concerns a shared sense of responsibility. Both provide questions for examining commitment and teamwork.

How the analysis works

Target

Define what is felt to be owned before writing items.

Level

Keep individual ownership and collective ownership separate.

Pathway

Commitment and cooperation are relationships to test with new data.

Model specification

Individual / collective ownership → commitment → cooperation

The model is a conceptual specification. Numerical coefficients remain unestimated.

Research framework

A conceptual framework for relationships to test in an organizational setting.

Proposed antecedents

Control + identification

The framework links control over work, identification and responsibility with a felt ownership connection. The current source does not provide an estimated measurement model.

Concept

Individual ownership

A felt sense that a work target is mine. It is distinct from legal ownership and from a verified claim of higher performance.

Concept

Collective ownership

A shared sense that the target is ours. Team-level meaning requires a justified measure and aggregation; it cannot be assumed from one person's answer.

Proposed mediator

Commitment

The framework proposes commitment as a link to cooperative behavior. No coefficients, sample or confirmed mediation test are supplied.

Proposed outcome

Cooperation

Define observable cooperative behavior before collecting data. This conceptual endpoint does not establish employee retention or teaching improvement.

Proposed next study

Build the measures before testing the pathway

Develop a setting-specific ownership study with individual and team levels kept distinct.

Question
How do individual and shared ownership relate to later commitment and cooperation in a defined workplace?
Design & unit
Qualitative adaptation, independent measurement study and a longitudinal employee panel; teams are a possible second level.
Measures & analysis
Specify the ownership target, audit item meaning, test measurement structure and justify aggregation before structural modeling.
Data & sample
Requires a named setting, access, new measures and consent. Plan employees and independent teams separately, including attrition.
Decision gate
Do not estimate a team model without adequate team-level information. The existing page remains a conceptual framework.

Future university research

Universities can use the framework to frame interviews or future surveys about participation in decisions, the meaning of work and shared goals.

The current materials do not provide a study sample, model fit or university-specific effect sizes. The pathway is conceptual.
