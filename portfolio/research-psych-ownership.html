Psychological ownership at university

This framework examines how a sense that work is mine or ours may relate to commitment and cooperation in universities.

Explore the findings ↓Methods & sources ↗
Focus
Personal + shared ownership
Context
Vietnamese universities
Type
Framework to test

Personal or shared ownership

Proposed links to commitment and cooperation.

Separates personal ownership from shared responsibility to frame questions about commitment and cooperation.

How a university could use the framework

Plan interviews or a survey about decision-making, meaningful work and shared goals.

What the study does not establish

This is a research framework. The materials do not provide a university sample or measured effects.

Research design

Research planning · Concept development · Framework design

    Define the work

    Specify what a person or group feels connected to.

  2. 2

    Separate mine from ours

    Distinguish personal ownership from a shared sense of responsibility.

  3. 3

    Plan a study

    Collect new data to examine how these feelings relate to commitment and cooperation.
