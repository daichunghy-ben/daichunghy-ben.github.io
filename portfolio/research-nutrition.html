VIEN

Student diet quality

A survey of 280 university students examined nutrition knowledge, physical activity and the foods eaten on the previous day.

Explore the findings ↓Methods & sources ↗
Sample
280 students
Measures
Food knowledge, activity + diet
Design
One-time survey

Calorie knowledge lagged

Dietary guidance scored higher.

Compared students’ nutrition knowledge, activity and previous-day food intake, accounting for age and sex.

What a university could learn

Use the knowledge gaps to choose nutrition-education topics. Study the activity–diet relationship further.

What the study does not establish

This was a survey at one point in time. It cannot show that a class or an activity programme caused healthier eating.

How the study was conducted

Survey analysis · Multiple regression · CoNKS · IPAQ-SF · DQQ

  1. 1

    Ask about food knowledge

    The adapted CoNKS questionnaire has 20 questions: 7, 7 and 6 across its three topic areas.

  2. 2

    Ask about activity and diet

    IPAQ-SF is a short physical-activity questionnaire. DQQ asks about 29 food groups.

  3. 3

    Compare the answers

    The analysis checks how the answers vary together while accounting for age, sex, knowledge and activity. The reported model fit is R² = 0.538.
