## Final prompts

### virtual

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide portfolio research cover. Create a refined editorial 3D still life about virtual influencer persuasion: an elegant unbranded smartphone standing at an angle, with a fictional young adult female digital creator portrait on screen, wearing headphones, and a second small sculptural human-profile silhouette nearby. No interface labels, no social media logos, no words, no charts or numbers. Warm off-white studio background, matte charcoal phone, restrained dusty plum and pale lavender accents, lifelike polished materials, soft directional daylight, crisp shadows, art-directed composition filling the frame without excessive empty space. Mature professional visual, not neon, not cartoon stock art. Landscape composition, subject centered, suitable for a compact research hero beside English typography. This is a conceptual illustration, not a real experimental stimulus or researcher portrait.

### ev

Create an art-directed premium editorial 3D product scene: one elegant unbranded silver compact electric car in three-quarter view beside a restrained matte-blue charging post; a short curved roadway and two small geometric route markers suggest range and charging choices. Warm off-white studio setting, pale concrete floor, muted steel blue and small burnt-orange accents, soft natural daylight, precise materials and crisp gentle shadows. Tight landscape composition with the car clearly visible and filling most of the frame, minimal professional product photography appearance. No logos, no brand-specific car grille, no words, no charts, no numeric displays, no neon cityscape, no extra vehicles. This is an illustrative research cover, not a tested car or a result.
