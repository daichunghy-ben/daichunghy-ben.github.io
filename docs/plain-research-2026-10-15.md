# Research pages for readers without a methods background

Scope: all 11 research cases, plus the wording in the EV and Hotel experiences.
The site remains in English and retains the existing portfolio design.

The introduction now describes the work in everyday terms. Initial skill and
fact labels avoid unexplained method acronyms. Results state what was observed,
and practical use and limitations appear before the figures and statistics.
Captions explain how to read amounts, uncertainty, group shares and model output.
The three method steps use plain headings and explain named methods when needed.

Scientific detail stays visible lower on the page. No accordions, required
downloads or new interactions are added. Original model diagrams, equations,
technical source notes, proposed-study plans and publication statuses stay intact.
No new causal, commercial, data-collection or publication claim is introduced.

The EV exercise still uses the same three illustrative pairs. The Hotel map
still uses the original figure and preserves its numeric cluster IDs. No verified
crosswalk to named profiles is claimed.

Validation compares the changed evidence records against a before snapshot:
all fields except titles, captions and method explanations are identical.
Model records and interactive values are also unchanged. Browser checks cover
the reading order and visible content on all 11 cases at desktop and mobile
widths, and the EV/Hotel controls after the copy changes.

Release checks and receipts are in `.audit/plain-research-2026-10-15/`.
